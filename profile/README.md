<h3 align="center">Meldo</h3>

<p align="center">
  A free, open-source school planner for students in Germany that counts how often you raise your hand in class.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <em>Für Schülerinnen und Schüler: Meldungen zählen, Stundenplan, Noten, Hausaufgaben und Klausuren. Ohne Konto, ohne Werbung, ohne Tracking.</em>
</p>

Besides the hand-raise counter, Meldo has a timetable (with optional WebUntis import), grades on the 1–6 and 0–15 scales, homework and exams. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your device, and the optional Google Drive backup is encrypted on the device before it is uploaded.

| Repository | |
|---|---|
| [meldo-ios](https://github.com/Meldo-dev/meldo-ios) | The iPhone and iPad app, written in SwiftUI |

| [meldo-core](https://github.com/Meldo-dev/meldo-core) | The Rust core both apps share: grades, timetable, WebUntis, statistics |
